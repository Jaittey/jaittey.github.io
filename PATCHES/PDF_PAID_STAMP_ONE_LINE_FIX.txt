Optional salary-slip compatibility fix for the current repository
===============================================================

File:
src/services/pdf.js

Find:
const stamp = settings.companyStampDataUrl || settings.paidStampDataUrl || await loadPaidStampAsDataUrl();

Replace with:
const stamp = settings.paidStampDataUrl || await loadPaidStampAsDataUrl();

Why:
Company Stamp and Paid Stamp are now separate assets. Paid salary slips should use the Paid Stamp, not the Company Stamp.

The main Invoice/Quotation PDF service included in this update is already fixed.
